## What the service does

All generation happens on zombietrend.org. You upload two photos: the first is the survivor, the second is the person or pet who "turns". You can pick a flashback memory, a length (8, 12 or 15 seconds) and add a short optional note. We then:

1. **Create a scene preview.** Your photos and note are sent to our AI processing provider to compose a cinematic, non-graphic still of the two subjects in a post-apocalyptic scene, with the second subject styled as a zombie. The first preview each day is free and carries a watermark.
2. **Generate the video.** If you continue, we turn the scene into a short vertical (9:16) story with sound: the survivor hesitates, the zombie hugs instead of biting, and the clip cuts to a warm memory of the two of you. The MP4 is usually ready in 3-5 minutes. Each video uses credits; the exact amount is shown on the button before you start.

Finished videos are saved in **My videos** in your account and can be downloaded without a watermark. AI output varies: check faces, hands and the flashback before you share a result. We do not guarantee that a generation will match your expectations or look exactly like the people or pets in your photos.
